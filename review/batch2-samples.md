## Decisions to approve

1. **Small closed sets.** `tools/validate.js` required 15 answers per prompt and every tier filled. Several of your closed sets are smaller (armor of God 6, churches 7, cross sayings 7, I am sayings 8). The validator now allows closed sets down to 6 answers; they still need a Mustard Seed and exactly one Lost Sheep, and empty middle tiers become warnings.
2. **"Open" prompts that can't reach 60.** New names (30) and giants (17) are near-exhaustive from the text but well short of 60, so they are marked closed. Every closed set counts toward the one-closed-set-per-day rule.
3. **New names: Lost Sheep changed to Pashhur / Magor-missabib** (Jeremiah 20:3). Because either name is accepted, Zaphenath-paneah would also accept "Joseph", one of the most common names typed, for 100 points. Pashhur is a renaming where both names are rare. Joseph stays in the bank at Lost Coin.
4. **New names: left out on purpose.** Uzziah / Azariah (the alias "Azariah" collides with Abednego's Hebrew name), Levi / Matthew and Nathanael / Bartholomew (the text never says "also called"; they are near misses, worded neutrally), Hosea's children Lo-ruhamah / Ruhamah (the WEB translates the names), and Ish-bosheth / Esh-baal (only inferred from genealogies).
5. **Raised from the dead: two witnesses added** (Revelation 11:11, "the breath of life from God entered into them"). The note says it is in John's vision and takes no position on interpretation. Say if you'd rather leave it out.
6. **"Widow's son" is a near miss** that asks for the town, since two widows' sons were raised (Zarephath and Nain).
7. **"Giants" on its own is a near miss.** The KJV's "giants" in Genesis 6:4 is the WEB's Nephilim, and accepting the bare word would give Nephilim points for restating the prompt.
8. **New categories:** "Signs & Wonders" and "Words of Jesus". New names joins "Names & Titles"; giants joins "People".

## Seed references corrected so far

- **Acts 4:36:** Barnabas's earlier name is **Joses** in both the WEB and KJV, not Joseph. Corrected.
- **Ishbi-benob:** the WEB spells it **Ishbibenob** (2 Samuel 21:16); both spellings are accepted.
- **Arba:** the WEB's Joshua 14:15 says only "Kiriath Arba, after the greatest man among the Anakim"; the name Arba is in the KJV there ("which Arba was a great man"), and in both at Joshua 15:13.
- **Og (Deuteronomy 3:11):** the WEB says "Rephaim" and the KJV "giants". Both are fine; noted so the Rephaim answer cites the same verse.
- **The six-fingered man (2 Samuel 21:20):** the text says "a man of great stature, who had six fingers on every hand". The answer is "Six fingers", with "six-fingered man (of Gath)" and "man with six fingers" as accepted forms (listed for hand review in `tools/reports/forms.txt`).
- All other seed references in these five prompts were found as given.
