    How to fish

    “Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men.” Matthew 4:19 (KJV)

    1. Each day brings one Daily Cast: seven prompts from the Bible, the same for everyone. A new cast starts at midnight Eastern (New York time).
    2. You have 25 seconds per prompt. Type an answer and press Cast. Wrong guesses cost nothing: keep casting until something lands.
    3. Close spellings get a did you mean. Cast again to confirm, or edit.
    4. Every prompt has many right answers. The rarer your answer, the more it scores.
    5. Each point sinks the net 10 metres. A perfect day reaches 7,000 m.
    6. If the clock runs out first, nothing landed: +0, and on to the next cast.
