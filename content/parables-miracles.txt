? Vine and Branches; true vine | One of the I am sayings (John 15:1-8), usually listed apart from the parables.
? Ewe Lamb; Nathan | That is a parable, but Nathan told it to David (2 Samuel 12:1-4).
? Resurrection; his resurrection; empty tomb; Easter | The greatest sign of all, but this prompt covers the miracles Jesus worked during his ministry.
? Transfiguration; transfigured | A revelation of his glory (Matthew 17:1-8), usually listed apart from his miracles.
? Virgin birth; ascension | A wonder of his life, but not a miracle he worked during his ministry.
? Red Sea; manna; burning bush; plagues | That belongs to the story of Moses.
? Pentecost; tongues of fire | That came after his ascension (Acts 2).
? healed the sick; raised the dead; miracles | That covers many miracles. Which one do you have in mind?
